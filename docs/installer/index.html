Sleep Tracker installer

Flash the firmware, hand over your WiFi, and land on the device's web app — without touching a terminal. Plug in your ESP32-C3 board over USB and click below.

Your browser does not support flashing over USB. Use desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera. This page must be served over HTTPS for browser flashing to work. Open the GitHub Pages copy. Chrome / Edge / Opera on desktop · Chrome on Android.

What this does

  1. Detects your ESP32-C3 over its native USB serial port.
  2. Flashes the merged firmware image (~4 MB) to the board.
  3. Picks your home WiFi from a scan and sends it to the device over the same USB cable using the Improv-Serial protocol.
  4. Redirects you to the device's local web UI once it joins your network — typically http://sleep-tracker.local/.

Wiring

One sensor, four wires:

MAX30102 VIN3V3 pin
MAX30102 GNDGND
MAX30102 SDAGPIO 4
MAX30102 SCLGPIO 5
